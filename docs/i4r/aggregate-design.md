# Secondary analysis of cached annual citation totals

Recorded October 6, 2026, before calculating these matched contrasts. Some target annual counts and the existing citation analyses have already been examined. This is a retrospective extension, not a preregistration.

The estimand is the equal-article average of the change in citations from year −1 to year +1 after a verified material-error disclosure, minus the corresponding change among matched controls, for the cases supported by the cached journal pools. The outcome is OpenAlex's precomputed annual total across citing document types. It differs from the primary deduplicated article/review measure. The primary analysis remains pending complete incoming-link retrieval.

Use the existing exposure, age, retraction, journal, publication-year, text-distance, citation-caliper and weighting rules. Require complete annual totals for every otherwise eligible candidate before matching. All-type pre-period counts determine these matches, so this is a separately matched secondary analysis, not an outcome substitution holding the primary matches fixed. Freeze the public annual inputs and matches before inspecting the matched contrasts. Do not relax calipers after observing support or results.

Extract only exact verified work identities from cached responses with source URLs, retrieval timestamps and hashes. Conflicting snapshots or multiple unresolved OpenAlex identities cannot be summed. A valid `counts_by_year` list supports absent-year zeros only within the documented recent window: the retrieval year minus nine through the last completed year. Require a work update in the retrieval year; older snapshots remain unavailable for this comparison. Values outside the supported window remain missing even if an occasional older positive bin exists. See OpenAlex's [work attributes](https://help.openalex.org/data/works/attributes/) and [common attributes](https://help.openalex.org/data/common-attributes/). These totals are precomputed and may differ from a live query; they cannot provide citation-level deduplication or document-type filtering.

Check all-type totals against available complete article/review histories on the same paper-years. All-type totals should generally be at least as large, but snapshot drift, aliases and database updates can cause discrepancies requiring investigation. Report the comparison rather than treating either measurement as ground truth. Use the same measurement in treated and control arms.

Report individual matched contrasts, mean and median citation levels, their equal-treated-weight average, pre-period paths, and leave-one-disclosure-out results. Reuse the declared longer-horizon and baseline sensitivities on supported cohorts. Clustered intervals are exploratory conditional summaries: a handful of disclosures cannot sustain confident causal inference. Parallel counterfactual trends, no anticipation, and no differential coincident shocks remain assumptions; selection into this available subset limits generalization. Total citations do not measure endorsement of the erroneous claim.

Do not pool these absolute contrasts with the existing proportional cross-audit synthesis as if they measured the same quantity. Retain this study separately until a compatible, substantively justified synthesis is available.
