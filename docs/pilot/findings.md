# Pilot feasibility results

The pilot selected 20 original papers and sampled 250 citation relationships: 78 pre-warning and 172 post-warning. Independent claim verification and citation coding are pending; there is no estimate of continued reliance.

Automated retrieval obtained files for 105/250 relationships (42.0%). Of these, 57 have an XML article body and a matching article DOI; 48 are PDFs awaiting identity and readability checks. File acquisition is an upper bound on verified readable coverage. The 80% availability criterion is not met by the present retrieval.

Next steps are targeted full-text acquisition and two independent readers. Keep the sample fixed; do not replace inaccessible papers.
